03 / Behind adsuhari

Business brain.
Design eye.
Builder instinct.

I’m Adika Suhari — a CMO and entrepreneur who likes turning interesting problems into internet products. Before that, I worked as a freelance photographer, which is probably why I care as much about visual culture and design as I do about growth and business.

adsuhari is where those things meet. It gives me a home for ideas that would otherwise look unrelated — without forcing them into one niche.
